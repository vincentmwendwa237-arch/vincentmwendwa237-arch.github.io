About Finney Vincent

Finney Vincent is an electrical engineer by profession, gospel minister, author and Christian mentor. He is the Founder and Mentor of Phlogizo Clock Mentorship Group, a discipleship community established in January 2026 to help believers grow in spiritual fire, prayer, discipline, accountability and commitment to God's purpose.

His work brings together professional development, Christian discipleship, Bible teaching, prayer and practical mentorship, with an emphasis on keeping believers spiritually alert and committed to their walk with Christ.
